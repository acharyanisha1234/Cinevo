# 🎬 Cinevo - Movie Streaming Platform

Cinevo is a modern movie streaming platform where you can watch movies online for free. Browse thousands of movies, add them to your favorites, track your watch history, and share with friends.

**Live Demo:** [Coming Soon](#)

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Watch Movies
- Watch movies online with smooth video playback
- Continue watching from where you left off
- High-quality video streaming
- Works on all devices (mobile, tablet, desktop)

### 🔍 Browse & Discover
- **Trending Now** - Most popular movies today
- **Popular** - Movies everyone is watching
- **Top Rated** - Highest rated movies
- **Now Playing** - Currently in theaters
- **Upcoming** - Coming soon
- **Search** - Find any movie by title

### ❤️ Favorites
- Add movies to favorites with one click
- Heart button animation
- Your favorites saved automatically
- Dedicated favorites page
- Remove from favorites anytime

### 📺 Watch History
- Automatically saves your watch progress
- Resume watching from any device
- Shows percentage completed
- "Watched" badge for completed movies
- Clear history option

### 📱 Social Sharing
- Share movies on:
  - Facebook
  - Twitter/X
  - WhatsApp
  - Instagram
  - Email
- Copy movie link to share anywhere
- Native share on mobile devices

### 🔐 User Account
- Create account with email
- Login / Logout
- Your favorites and history saved
- Profile management
- Secure authentication

### 🎨 Beautiful Design
- Dark theme for comfortable viewing
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Responsive on all screen sizes
- Professional movie poster display
- Easy to navigate
